Final Project

The final project is the centrepiece of this module (100% of grade). You will experiment and build a complete AI system demonstrating your ability to address a narrow research question and design and implement a foundational model-based applications.

Project Topics

A list of projects will be shared on Canvas. You may select one of the provided topics and outline your strategy in your proposal. If you prefer to pursue your own idea, you must obtain the instructor's permission beforehand via. email.

Requirements

Projects must completed individually. Each project must address 2-3 interesting small research questions by conducting extensive experiments. An example small research question would be understanding role of model size and context size on performance for your problem. Your project must utilize all of the following:

  • Prompt engineering and in-context learning OR Appropriate Context Construction
  • Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) OR OR Agentic side (tool use, planning, etc.) OR Multimodal (vision, audio)
  • Fine-tuning (LoRA, instruction tuning, RLHF)
  • Local Inference (APIs, optimisation, monitoring)
  • A Small Front End for Demo
